4 · Statistics explained

Kaplan-Meier estimator computes survival probability at each event time as a running product of conditional survival fractions. 95% CI uses Greenwood's formula.

Log-rank test compares survival distributions non-parametrically. Under H0 (no difference), the test statistic follows chi-squared with df = k-1.

Cox proportional hazards model quantifies the hazard ratio (HR) via partial likelihood maximization (Newton-Raphson). HR > 1 = higher risk than reference; HR < 1 = protective.
